# RoosterWorks Station Modules — Roadmap

**Published stable version:** v0.7.5 (October 14, 2026).

## Completed through v0.7.5

- Independent ring colors, exterior finishes and logistics storage choices.
- Full 5 m orbital-station expansion with adapter.
- Compact habitats; long crew tubes; laboratory and command storage options.
- Editor improvements and in-game-tested stable release.
